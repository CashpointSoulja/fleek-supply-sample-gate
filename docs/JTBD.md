# Jobs to be done

## Main job
**When** a new supplier sends a sample for a category we are testing, **I want to** turn what the inspector saw into a clear reject / more evidence / small pilot call, **so I can** avoid a bad first batch without killing a supplier who could become repeatable supply.

## Related jobs
| When… | I want to… | So I can… | Feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| the sample is small or partly photographed | see exactly what is missing | send one specific request instead of a vague "more info please" | Missing evidence list |
| the supplier quotes stock in kg or bales | force a pieces conversion | compare like with like | Unit gate + conversion field |
| a sample looks great | check that existing buyers want it at this price | stop quality alone pushing a bet forward | Demand-fit checklist |
| I approve a test | cap it before anyone gets excited | keep the downside small and known | Pilot ceiling |
| the bet is reviewed later | show what I knew when I decided | defend or revisit the call honestly | Decision log, fingerprints, stale flag, export |
| an inspector sends a spreadsheet | load it without retyping | move quickly but safely | Strict CSV import |

## Emotional / social jobs
- Feel confident saying "not yet" to a supplier the team likes.
- Be seen by leadership as moving fast **and** disciplined with money.

## Anti-jobs (deliberately not done)
- Negotiating, ordering or paying.
- Contacting suppliers or buyers.
- Forecasting the category's GMV from a sample.

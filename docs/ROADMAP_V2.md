# v2 roadmap (ideas, not commitments)

| Priority | Item | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Back-test thresholds against real sample → first-batch outcomes | Replace guesses (A4) with calibrated defaults |
| 2 | First-batch inspection screen tied to the pilot entry | Measure the north-star acceptance gap directly |
| 3 | Link demand checks to marketplace data (buyer segment, price band, sell-through) | Remove self-reporting (A8) |
| 4 | Grade tiers and defect codes (stain, zip, fade, size skew) | Richer failure reasons and supplier feedback |
| 5 | Sequential sampling: "inspect N more to decide" | Cheapest path out of "more evidence" |
| 6 | Landed-cost calculator (freight, duty, FX) | Better cost gate than sample price |
| 7 | Shared workspace, roles and server-side append-only log | Team use and real audit |
| 8 | Supplier scorecard across categories and re-samples | Track repeatability over time |
| 9 | Photo upload with per-piece linkage | Turn photo count into evidence you can open |

Still out of scope in v2: automatic outreach, purchasing and payments.
